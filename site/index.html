How it works

Three steps to a readable page

1

Install the extension

Add OpenTranslate to Safari on your Mac, iPhone or iPad and enable it for the sites you read.

2

Pick a provider

Start free with Google or Microsoft, or paste an API key for DeepL or any of 28 LLM engines.

3

Open & translate

Tap the popup on any page. Read the translation on its own, or side-by-side with the original.

Features

Everything you need to read the whole web

A serious translation engine wrapped in a native Safari extension.

~32 translation providers

Free Google & Microsoft engines, DeepL / DeepLX, plus 28 LLM providers via the Vercel AI SDK. Switch anytime.

Bring your own LLM

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama and more — your keys, your models, your bill.

Bilingual or translation-only

Render the translation in place, or keep the original alongside it to check the source as you read.

Popup & full settings

A one-tap popup for quick control, and a complete settings page to fine-tune providers, languages and rendering.

Native to Safari on iPhone & iPad

A real Safari Web Extension for iOS / iPadOS — and macOS too. Not a wrapper. It behaves the way you expect.

Free & open source

GPL-3.0-or-later. Read every line, build it yourself, or contribute. No paywall, no lock-in, ever.

Privacy first

100% local. Nothing phones home.

Your settings, keys and language preferences live on your device and only your device. When you translate, the page text goes straight to the provider you chose — never through us, because there is no us to go through.

No account
No sign-up, no email, no login wall.
No cloud sync
Settings stored locally on each device.
Zero telemetry
No analytics, no tracking, no beacons.
